Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

What magikdex stores

  • An anonymous account. The first time you open magikdex it creates an account with a random id and no name, so your brews have somewhere to live.
  • Your dex. The legends you add, the cards in each brew, and the names and tags you give them.
  • An email address, only if you add one. It is used to send you a sign-in code so you can get your dex back on another device. It is used for nothing else.
  • Settings on your device. Things like your default sort and the last brew you opened are kept in your browser's storage.

Accounts and decks are stored with Supabase, the database service magikdex runs on.

What magikdex does not do

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone.

Other services your device talks to

  • Scryfall serves card data and card images directly to your device.
  • Moxfield and Archidekt: if you import a deck by link, magikdex's server fetches that public deck for you.
  • Bracket Card: if you send a brew there, the list travels in the link itself and is not uploaded by magikdex.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose delete my data. That permanently removes every brew and legend in your dex and signs you out.
